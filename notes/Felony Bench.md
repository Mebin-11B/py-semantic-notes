## Methodology

    

Felony Bench counts unique instances where AI agents affect third-party entities. Escaping a sandbox alone does not constitute a counted incident. It is for these reasons that Frontier Security's [Kimi K3](https://blog.frontier.security/chinese-model-kimi-k3-breaks-uk-ai-safety-institute-benchmark-evaluations/) incident and [Alibaba's ROME](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.24873) incident are not counted.
