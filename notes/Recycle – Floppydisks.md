# [Recycle – Floppydisks](https://www.floppydisk.com/recycle)

## Digest Summary
The Floppydisk Recycle Program accepts 3.5‑inch floppy disks of any quantity. For shipments exceeding 200 disks, the program provides a small shipping rebate, contingent on inclusion of the provided reimbursement form. All disks should be mailed to: Floppydisk Recycle Program, 668 North Coast Hwy #1117, Laguna Beach, CA 92651. The reimbursement form outlines the rebate eligibility criteria; those rules are detailed on the form itself. The program does not specify minimum or maximum quantities beyond the rebate threshold, and there are no additional processing fees mentioned. Participants are instructed to include the completed form with the shipment to qualify for the offset. The site includes three images: a 1.44 MB floppy disk, recycled floppy disks, and a PDF of the 2026 reimbursement form.
