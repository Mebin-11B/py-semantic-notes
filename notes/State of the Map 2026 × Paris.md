# State of the Map 2026

            

State of the Map is the annual event for all mappers and OpenStreetMap users. In 2026 the State of the Map conference will be taking place in Paris and online. It will be a three day conference packed with talks, workshops, discussion rounds and more.

            

Your organising team.

            

## Attending

            

              If you are attending the conference either in person in Paris or online, here are some useful links to find information:
              
                
- How to get to the [venue](https://2026.stateofthemap.org/venue/)
                
- [Programme](https://2026.stateofthemap.org/programme/)
                
- Programme for your [mobile devices](https://sotm.osmz.ru/sotm2026.html)
                
- Connect with other attendees on our chat rooms on [Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/!uOhylXbXUNhCIpMWkx:matrix.org) or [Telegram](https://t.me/sotm_international), and follow us on our social media channels, e.g. [Mastodon](https://en.osm.town/@sotm).
                
                
- [Social Event](https://2026.stateofthemap.org/programme/social_event/) information
                
- Conference [Posters](https://2026.stateofthemap.org/posters/)
                
- [Live Streams](https://2026.stateofthemap.org/stream/) – Note that if you have a Venueless ticket, please use the link in your ticket to get to the interactive page with live streams, possibility to ask questions, break-out rooms, etc.
