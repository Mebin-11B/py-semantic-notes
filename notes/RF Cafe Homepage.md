Norton's Theorem Quiz

[Norton's theorem](https://www.rfcafe.com/miscellany/quizzes/rf-cafe-quiz-95-norton-theorem.htm) is the dual counterpart 
to Thévenin’s theorem, offering another powerful method for simplifying complex 
linear circuits. It states that any linear two-terminal network can be reduced to 
an equivalent current source (IN) in parallel with a single resistance 
(RN). The Norton current equals the short-circuit current flowing through 
a direct connection across the terminals, while the Norton resistance is calculated 
by killing all independent sources - shorting voltage sources and opening current 
sources - then finding the equivalent resistance. This theorem shines when analyzing 
circuits with current sources in parallel, as it naturally simplifies current-divider
