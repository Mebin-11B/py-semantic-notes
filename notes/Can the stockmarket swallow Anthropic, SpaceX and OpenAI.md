## Discussion Summary
The discussion centers on the unusually large IPO valuations of SpaceX, Anthropic and similar firms, emphasizing rule changes that force massive passive‑investment inflows and raise concerns about overvaluation, market legitimacy, and the ability of the economy to absorb such capital without clear gains in living standards. Commenters note the potential short‑term demand from index funds and retail investors but question long‑term benefits, the sustainability of revenue models, and the risk of inflated prices outweighing economic value. Overall sentiment is skeptical of the broader impact despite acknowledging high investor interest.
