#### **Preorder Details**

The Bean Pointing Stick is currently in a preorder phase. That means that **any orders you place with us that include a Bean Pointing Stick will be shipped out on the basis of availability**.

The preorder is split into different tiers:

* EARLY ACCESS: Orders from this tier will ship out immediately.
* TIER A: Orders from this tier will ship out within 8 weeks of our launch date.
* TIER B: Orders from this tier will ship out within 20 weeks of our launch date.
* TIER C: Orders from this tier will ship out after 20 weeks of our launch date. Due to the large number of preorders in the earlier tiers, we do not have a specific timeline for when units from Tier C will ship. We will provide regular updates on our social media channels about the status of our preorder and will state when we project shipping Tier C preorders as soon as we can reasonably do so.

The launch date for the Bean Pointing Stick is 6 May 2026.

Ploopy has a strong history of preorder management. We have released multiple products under these exact preorder conditions, and we have always successfully delivered all units within the promised amount of time.

#### **Included with every Bean Pointing Stick**

*3D-printed parts (black)*

*Electronics*

* Bean Pointing Stick PCB
* USB-A to USB-C cable (optional)

*Hardware*

* 1x Screw
* 1x Magnet
* 4x Friction Pads
* 1x Friction Nub

[You can find full assembly instructions here](https://ploopyco.github.io/bean-pointing-stick). But…you don’t need them, since this comes fully-assembled.

#### **Default firmware**

The Bean Pointing Stick ships with QMK preloaded. Currently, [we’re shipping with our version of QMK](https://github.com/ploopyco/qmk_firmware/tree/ploopyco/bean).

**Want to load new firmware onto your Ploopy device?** Loading new firmware is fast and easy, and doesn’t require anything except your computer. Check out our guide [here](https://ploopyco.github.io/bean-pointing-stick/appendices/programming/). It includes a full set of instructions on loading new firmware. You can load new firmware with tools like QMK Toolbox or the QMK Configurator.

**A note for macOS users:** macOS does smoothing for input devices automatically. This interferes with the code that runs on the Bean, meaning that pixel-by-pixel high-resolution scrolling doesn’t work on macOS. Getting usable scrolling to work on macOS requires about 10 seconds of extra work; [see here for full details](https://github.com/ploopyco/bean-pointing-stick/tree/main/firmwares).

#### **Modding**

Planning on modifying your device? Read through [the official Modder’s Guide](https://blog.ploopy.co/modders-guide) first for helpful tips!

#### **Checkout page not working?**

Sometimes, ad-blocking software will interfere with the checkout process. It’ll result in the checkout page hanging forever after you’ve entered your payment information. If this is happening to you, try temporarily disabling your ad-blockers.
