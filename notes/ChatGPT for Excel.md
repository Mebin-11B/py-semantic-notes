## Discussion Summary
The comments express widespread disappointment with current AI features for spreadsheets and presentation tools, citing poor performance, inaccuracy, slow response times, and limited editing capabilities in Google Sheets, Excel Copilot, and ChatGPT for Excel. Users highlight security worries about data sharing, frequent errors, and the difficulty of integrating AI into specialized workflows. Some praise Claude‑generated PowerPoint decks and see potential in broader AI agents, yet the prevailing view is that existing implementations are unreliable, cumbersome, and not yet ready for serious professional use.
