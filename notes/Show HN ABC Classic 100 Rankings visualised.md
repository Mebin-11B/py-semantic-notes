# [Show HN: ABC Classic 100 Rankings visualised](https://classic100.gotski.workers.dev/)

## Digest Summary
ABC Classic FM’s “Classic 100” annual poll results from 2001‑2021 are visualized as a dot‑and‑line chart. Each dot marks a specific composition; connecting lines show how that work’s rank shifted across the three surveyed years (2001, 2010, 2021). Interactive features allow users to select a dot, highlighting the chosen piece and related works by the same composer. The visualization illustrates trends such as “The Lark Ascending,” which ranked #2 in 2001, fell in 2010, and rose again by 2021. The page also contains a brief author note unrelated to the data.

ABC Classic 100 Rankings: 2001 to 2021
--------------------------------------

Every year since 2001, ABC Classic FM has run a
[Classic 100](https://www.abc.net.au/classic/classic100/archive/)
event, where listeners vote on their favourite pieces.

This page visualises the rankings for three different years:

Each dot 
represents a piece of music.

The lines track how the rankings for that piece have changed between 2001 and 2021.

You can **click or tap on a dot to highlight** that piece and show detailed information.

Other pieces by the **same composer** will also highlight in a lighter shade.

My favourite observation from this visualisation is that
'The Lark Ascending' started high in the rankings (#2) in 2001, descended
in 2010, before ascending again in 2021.
