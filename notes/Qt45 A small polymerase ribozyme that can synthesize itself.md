> **One-line summary:** Researchers discovered a remarkably small 45-nucleotide polymerase ribozyme (named Qt45) capable of catalyzing general RNA-templated synthesis of itself and its complementary strand under prebiotic conditions.
>
> **Key points:**
> - Qt45 is a newly discovered 45-nucleotide polymerase ribozyme, significantly smaller than previously known ribozymes.
> - The ribozyme catalyzes general RNA-templated RNA synthesis using trinucleotide triphosphate (triplet) substrates.
> - Crucially, it can synthesize both its complementary strand and an exact copy of itself under eutectic ice conditions.
> - Its small size dramatically reduces the mathematical barrier to the spontaneous emergence of self-replicating systems.
> - The finding supports the "RNA World" hypothesis, showing that self-sustained catalytic replication is more abundant in RNA sequence space than previously thought.
