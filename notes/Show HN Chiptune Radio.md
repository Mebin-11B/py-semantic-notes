# [Show HN: Chiptune Radio](https://chiptune-radio.alephvoid.com/)

## Digest Summary
The ChiptuneRadio service delivers a continuous, 24/7 broadcast of chiptune music—a genre that employs authentic 8‑bit sound‑chip synthesis, tracker‑based composition tools, and the aesthetic energy of the demoscene community. Operated by Aleph Void, LLC, the channel streams on Twitch, providing listeners with an uninterrupted audio feed that reflects the retro video‑game sound palette and the technical creativity associated with tracker software. The stream’s purpose is to maintain an around‑the‑clock availability of this niche electronic music style, targeting audiences seeking persistent access to classic chip‑generated compositions and demoscene‑inspired tracks. Viewers can join the channel at any time, and the stream runs without scheduled intermissions, ensuring continuous playback of curated playlists that feature both original productions and community‑submitted tracks. The service aligns with Aleph Void’s broader focus on preserving and promoting retro digital audio culture.

## What Is This?

      

Chiptune Radio is a side project of Aleph Void, LLC — the music of sound chips past, streaming while you work, reverse engineer, or just vibe.

### Pure Chiptune

          

Music composed for and inspired by classic sound hardware — the SID, the NES APU, the Game Boy's four channels, and the tracker scene that keeps them alive.
