## Digest Summary
The page “Bugs Apple Loves” presents a quantitative model for estimating the productivity loss caused by unresolved Apple software bugs. It defines four components:  

- **Base Impact** = Users Affected × Frequency × Time per Incident, measuring the direct time each affected user spends dealing with a bug.  
- **Power User Tax** = Σ (Workaround Time × Participation Rate), capturing additional effort from users who create or apply temporary fixes.  
- **Shame Multiplier** = Years Unfixed × Pressure Factor, scaling impact by the duration the bug has remained unresolved and its perceived urgency.  
- **Verdict** = Human Hours Wasted ÷ Engineering Hours to Fix, indicating how many times the wasted human labor exceeds the engineering effort required for a fix.  

The site invites readers to edit the underlying numbers, emphasizing that each bug’s parameters differ but the overall calculation remains real‑valued.
