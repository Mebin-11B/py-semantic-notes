Lightning-fast mouse control with the keyboard.

## People love Mouseless

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kaizkf_lXPI)

## Stay in Flow

Mouseless: click in as little as under a second, simply by typing a few coordinates.

Mouse: more than 2 seconds to grab the mouse and click, plus extra time to return to the keyboard.

## Complete Control

Every button: left, right, middle, back, and forward.

Every action: click, move, drag, drop, scroll, double and triple click.

## Mouse keys done right

Free mode allows for smooth, customizable movement without the overlay -- a modern upgrade to 'mouse keys' implementations built into the OS or custom keyboards.

## Health-saving Ergonomics

Repetitive mouse clicks or prolonged trackpad use can lead to serious pain and strain -- Mouseless protects your health and ability to work by distributing click-work across the keyboard.

## Beyond the Desk

Whether on the couch, in bed, or on the go, Mouseless gives you effortless cursor control, no surface required.

## Elevated Accessibility

People who can't use mice deserve an efficient, full-featured solution -- Mouseless is exactly that.
